# Cold Chain Spoilage Watch

Notices **chemical change in sealed storage** — which can precede a temperature logger
registering a problem.

- **Tier:** recipe.
- **Claim:** `spoilage-risk`, evidence linked.
- **Limits:** sealed storage only; reports change vs baseline — validate on your product.

## Why it's honest
We do not claim to identify spoilage organisms or report absolute VOC. We report a
drift-corrected change signal and its variability, and say so.
